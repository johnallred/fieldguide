# 02 Field enablement and coaching

Status: ready-for-agent

Category: `edu`. Asked for by the platform enablement posting.

Teaching the people who teach, sell and demo, rather than the end audience.

## Why it is not `workshops`

`workshops` designs the three-hour session against a thirty-four-hour path.
This is the layer above: practice sessions, office hours, and self-service
resources so the field can learn without you in the room.

## What the page has to cover

- The difference between enabling a learner and enabling a deliverer
- Practice sessions and feedback on delivery, not on content
- Self-service resources as the scaling mechanism, and how to tell when they work
- Carrying field signal back to product: where people get stuck is a finding
- `working-with-engineers` has no counterpart for go-to-market. This is it.
