# 03 Demo design

Status: ready-for-agent

Category: `edu` or `gp`. Asked for by the platform enablement posting.

## Why it is not `sample-apps`

A gallery is a list of clonable promises that break in two ways. A demo is
performed: time-boxed, aimed at a persona, built around a narrative beat. The
posting draws the line itself, between a demo that raises awareness and one
that creates champions.

## What the page has to cover

- What a demo optimises for that a sample does not
- Persona and industry variants from one spine
- The failure mode: a demo that only its author can run
- How a demo is tested, given that its success condition is a reaction
