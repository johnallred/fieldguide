    The whole thing, in order¶

    The learning paths are curated and opinionated. This page is the mechanical alternative: a topological sort of the prerequisite graph, so no concept appears before something it depends on.

    It is longer and less useful than a path. It is here because a complete ordering is the thing a reader asks for once they have decided to do all of it, and because generating it proves the graph has no cycles.
