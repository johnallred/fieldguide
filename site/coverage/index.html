    The page contract¶

    Every concept page answers the same seven questions in the same order, and CI rejects one that does not:

    1. What it is
    2. Why the job asks for it
    3. The golden path
    4. Reference
    5. How to talk about it
    6. How to prove it
    7. Related

    That is a content model applied to a learning corpus rather than to a product's documentation. The payoff is that the thirtieth page costs a reader nothing to navigate, because they learned the shape on the first.
